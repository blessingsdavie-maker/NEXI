Questions, answered

A safety tool
should be clear.

We are building Nexi carefully, with people — not surveillance — at the centre.

Create an account →
Can someone track me with Nexi?

No. Location sharing is optional, specific to a moment, and under your control. It is never public or switched on by default.

What happens if I miss a check-in?

Your chosen circle can receive a gentle prompt after the grace period you set. The goal is a conversation, not an alarm.

Does Nexi replace emergency services?

No. Nexi is a coordination tool for your trusted people. It does not replace emergency services or guarantee emergency response.

When will Nexi be available?

We are shaping the first Kenya launch with early members. Create an account to be part of the first release.
